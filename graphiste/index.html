01 — Approach

Design that speaks
before you read it

My visual work started with posters and flyers — a discipline that teaches you one brutal truth: you have half a second to capture attention, or you've lost it.

That constraint shaped how I think about every design decision. Every element earns its place. Every visual choice carries meaning. Nothing is decoration for its own sake.

I'm expanding into other formats — visual identity, digital design, editorial layouts — but I bring the same rigour: communication first, aesthetics second, and always both.

Based in Cotonou, Bénin. Available for remote projects worldwide.

Tools I use

What I believe about design
01
Clarity over cleverness. The most beautiful design is one that is instantly understood.
02
Context is everything. A poster for a Cotonou street wall and one for a Parisian gallery are not the same brief — even if they say the same words.
03
Less is harder than more. Restraint takes skill. Anyone can fill a page.
04
Good design is for someone. I always ask: who is this for, and what do we want them to feel?
